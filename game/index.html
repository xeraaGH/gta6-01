AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY
Steal cars. Outrun the Coral Bay PD. Take back the city — one job at a time.
A completely original game. Coral Bay, its people and its story are invented —
no Rockstar, GTA or other commercial game content. Plays offline, keyboard + mouse.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D — move / drive
Mouse — look (drag or click to lock)
Shift — sprint
Space — jump / handbrake
E — steal / enter / exit car
Click / F — shoot / punch
1–4 / hold Q — weapons
R — radio station
M — missions & phone
H — horn
P / Esc — pause
C — camera view

Follow the gold M marker to start the next story job. Crime raises your wanted stars — the Coral Bay PD sends patrol cars, roadblocks and, at 4 stars, the Air One helicopter. Lay low to cool off, or reach the safehouse. Earn cash from jobs, buy guns and armor at the TAC SHOP, tune your ride at the GARAGE.
